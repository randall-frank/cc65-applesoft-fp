Detailed Description

Applesoft ROM floating point math routines for the Apple II.

The system is designed around a a pair of virtual registers located in zero page memory, the FAC (Floating Accumulator) and ARG (Floating Argument). There are routines to load data into AS_FAC or the AS_ARG and to perform math operations on those registers.

Implements the math operation ARG = ARG ^ FAC. For example ARG ^ 0.5 is the square root of ARG.

◆ as_fp_swap_fac_arg()

void __fastcall__ as_fp_swap_fac_arg ( )
extern

Swap the contents of FAC and ARG.

The values of the FAC and ARG registers will be swapped. This can be useful to avoid unnecessary register load/store operations, for example to meet the ordering requirements of as_fp_arg_sub_fac() and as_fp_arg_div_fac().
