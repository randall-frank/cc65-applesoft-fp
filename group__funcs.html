Detailed Description

Applesoft ROM floating point math routines for the Apple II.

The system is designed around a virtual register located in zero page memory, the FAC (Floating Accumulator) and a floating point representation in RAM. Functions exist to copy the FAC to and from RAM (asfp_fac2mem(), asfp_mem2fac()). Common math functions can be performed directly on the FAC (e.g. sin, cos) or they can be performed on the FAC and a number in RAM (e.g. asfp_mem_mul_fac()).

Raise a value from memory to the power of the FAC and leave the result in the FAC.

Implements the math operation: FAC=mem^FAC. For example mem ^ 0.5 is the square root of mem.

asfp_str2fac("0.5");
asfp_mem_pow_fac(&AS_CONST_two);
